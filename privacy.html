Privacy Notice

Last updated: April 2026

Palmetto Staffing Agency, LLC (“Palmetto Staffing,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects the privacy of the job seekers and employer clients we work with. This notice explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you apply for a position or contact us through this website, we may collect:

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Employment information such as work history, education, skills, certifications, availability, and pay expectations.
  • Information needed for pre-employment screening, including references, background check results, drug screen results, and E-Verify status, where permitted by law.
  • Communications you send to us by phone, email, or through our online forms.

For employer clients, we collect business contact details and information about the staffing needs you describe to us.

How we use your information

  • To match job seekers with employer openings.
  • To verify identity and work eligibility, conduct screening, and complete onboarding paperwork.
  • To respond to your inquiries and to communicate with you about positions, applications, and assignments.
  • To meet legal, tax, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations.

How your information is stored

Applicant and client information submitted through our website forms is stored in secure third-party services that we use to operate our business, including our applicant tracking and form-submission tools. We limit access to this information to staff who need it to perform their jobs.

Sharing your information

We share information only as needed to provide staffing services, including:

  • With prospective employers when we are presenting you for a position.
  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as our applicant tracking and screening vendors, who are contractually required to protect your information.
  • When required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

Equal opportunity

Palmetto Staffing Agency is an Equal Opportunity Employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

Your choices

You can request a copy of the information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to the recordkeeping requirements that apply to staffing agencies. To make a request, contact our main office.
